Privacy Policy

Transparent business standards and clear client commitments.

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 · Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

At STUDIO CULT ("we", "our", or "us"), protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our clients, prospective partners, and website visitors is fundamental to our business ethos. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent practices regarding data collection, usage, and safeguarding when you visit our website (studiocult.in) or engage our creative, branding, website development, and digital marketing services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver exceptional marketing, design, and technical services. This may include:

  • Contact Information: Full name, business email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, company/school/hospital name, and physical location provided via our inquiry forms.
  • Project & Business Assets: Brand logos, color codes, high-resolution imagery, brand guidelines, product catalogs, and marketing briefs submitted for project execution.
  • Technical & Analytics Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and page navigation patterns collected anonymously via standard server logs and cookies.
  • Advertising Account Access: Delegated partner access to Meta Business Manager, Google Ads accounts, or Google Business Profiles granted for campaign setup and management. (We never store personal banking passwords).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate commercial and operational purposes, including:

  • Constructing customized quotations, proposals, and service scopes.
  • Developing and delivering creative design assets, social media posts, reels, websites, and advertising campaigns.
  • Communicating project milestones, review cycles, and performance reports.
  • Processing invoicing and accounting documentation.
  • Ensuring technical stability, security, and fraud prevention on our web assets.

4. Client Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

We treat all client marketing strategies, unreleased promotional materials, customer lists, and business metrics as strictly confidential. STUDIO CULT will never sell, rent, trade, or monetize your business or personal data to any third party. Access to client assets is strictly restricted to authorized team members directly working on your account.

5. Third-Party Platforms & Advertising Networks

When running paid ad campaigns, your business information interacts with third-party platforms such as Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google LLC, and YouTube. These platforms operate under their own independent privacy policies. Furthermore, advertising budgets are paid directly by you to these platforms via your own secured payment methods.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use standard cookies and tracking pixels to analyze web traffic and deliver an optimal browsing experience. You can choose to accept or decline cookies through your individual browser settings without affecting the core accessibility of our website.

7. Data Retention & Security

We implement robust industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration. Project assets and communication logs are retained only as long as necessary to service your account and fulfill legal accounting requirements.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices or wish to request data deletion, please contact our data team at:
