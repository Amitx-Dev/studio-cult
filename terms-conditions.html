Terms & Conditions

Transparent business standards and clear client commitments.

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 · Last Updated: November 2026

1. Scope of Agreement

These Terms & Conditions govern the provision of creative design, social media management, website development, and digital marketing services by STUDIO CULT ("Agency") to the client ("Client"). By approving an official quotation, invoice, or engaging our services, the Client agrees to be bound by these Terms.

2. Service Deliverables & Monthly Retainers

  • Advance Retainer Payments: Monthly retainer packages (Basic, Growth, Premium) require 100% advance payment at the start of each 30-day billing cycle before creative work or social scheduling commences.
  • Deliverable Timeframes: Deliverables (posts, reels, stories) allocated to a specific month must be utilized within that active 30-day billing cycle. Unused deliverables do not carry over or roll forward to subsequent months unless explicitly agreed in writing.
  • Content Approvals: The Client is responsible for timely review and feedback on draft creatives. Delays in Client approval do not extend the contractual monthly billing cycle.

3. Website Development Milestone Payments

Website design and custom development projects operate under the following strict milestone schedule:

  • 50% Advance Deposit: Required upon project initiation to begin UI/UX wireframing, architecture, and concept design.
  • 30% Progress Payment: Due immediately upon Client approval of the complete visual design mockups and before frontend/backend coding begins.
  • 20% Final Settlement: Due upon final quality assurance testing and prior to public domain deployment, hosting launch, or source code transfer.

4. Advertising Spend & Third-Party Ad Budgets

Agency management fees cover campaign strategy, creative design, copywriting, targeting, and weekly performance optimization. Ad spend budget is paid directly by the Client to Meta (Facebook/Instagram) or Google via the Client's own payment method. STUDIO CULT is not responsible for platform billing disputes, ad account restrictions caused by Client policy violations, or third-party platform algorithm fluctuations.

5. Additional Expenses & Third-Party Fees

Unless explicitly bundled in an approved written quote, the following items are billed separately:

  • Domain registration, web hosting renewal fees, and SSL certificates.
  • Third-party API subscriptions (e.g. WhatsApp Business Cloud API, premium SMS gateways).
  • On-site photography, videography equipment rentals, or professional talent casting.
  • Applicable government taxes and GST (18%).

6. Revisions & Scope Adjustments

All service packages include up to two (2) rounds of constructive revisions during the initial review phase. Requests for substantive changes to approved briefs, new deliverables outside the agreed scope, or additional revision rounds will be quoted and billed separately at our standard rate card.

7. Intellectual Property & Ownership

Upon receipt of full and final payment for all invoiced amounts, the Client is granted exclusive commercial ownership rights to the final delivered creative assets, logos, and custom website code. STUDIO CULT retains the right to display the final work in agency portfolios, case studies, and award submissions unless a formal Non-Disclosure Agreement explicitly prohibits it.

8. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

These terms shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any disputes arising under or in connection with these terms shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in India.
